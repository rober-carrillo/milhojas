What you can try today

Test it yourself

The website now has the landing page with its live demo, sign-in, and business setup that ends on your product list. Adding products comes next (P5). The Android app still shows only the placeholder home screen until P4.

Android APK

On GitHub: Actions → "Android APK" → latest run → Artifacts → milhojas-android-apk. Unzip it, send the .apk to your Android phone, and allow installing from this source when asked. A new APK builds automatically whenever the app changes. Each one is kept for 30 days.

Expo Go

Install Expo Go from Google Play and sign in. On your Mac, run git pull, corepack enable, pnpm install and pnpm dev:mobile, then scan the QR code. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi. Expo Go updates to SDK 58 in a few weeks. After that it won't open SDK 57 projects until the app is upgraded. The APK isn't affected.

Web

Open https://mismilhojas.vercel.app on any phone or computer. To sign in there, first do "Turn on sign-in emails in Supabase" above. On your Mac, pnpm dev:web runs it locally; with pnpm db:start the sign-in emails land at http://127.0.0.1:54324 and you can sign in as the demo vendor rosa@demo.milhojas.test.

Open decisions

Interviews vs. building

The plan said to talk to 15 vendors before writing app code. The code has started, so run the interviews in parallel and let them steer the screens above.

Buyer sign-in

Email link only, or also "Continuar con Google"? Both are free. Google is one tap for most Android users.

Email sender

Before real vendors sign in, Supabase needs an email sender of your own. Free options include a Gmail account with an app password (a few hundred a day) or a service like Resend, which needs a domain you own.

Source of truth: the rober-carrillo/milhojas repo. Budget ceiling: $100/month for year one; expected spend is about $400 total.
